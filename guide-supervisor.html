🎓 Supervisor Guide · دليل المشرف

MEDI 045 Anaesthesia Clinical Log Book · KSUMC

1 · Getting in

  1. Open the log book link and go to the registration screen.
  2. Press and hold the logo for two seconds. A staff entry button appears.
  3. Enter the supervisor PIN: 1234

The entry is deliberately hidden so students do not find it. The panel locks itself again after ten minutes of inactivity.

افتح شاشة التسجيل ، ثم اضغط مطوّلاً على الشعار ثانيتين، وأدخل رمز المشرف. المدخل مخفي عمداً عن الطلاب، وتُقفل اللوحة بعد عشر دقائق من الخمول.

2 · The dashboard

Three numbers sit at the top. Each one is a button. Tap it to see only those cases; tap it again to show everything.

  • To mark — submitted cases nobody has marked yet
  • Evaluated — cases already marked
  • Drafts — students have started but not submitted

At the foot of the dashboard, Result opens the results page and Admin opens the administration panel.

الأرقام الثلاثة أزرار: اضغط أي رقم لترى حالاته وحدها، واضغطه مرة ثانية لإلغاء التصفية.

3 · Marking a case

Open a case and press Mark this case. The panel has two pages.

Page 1 — Automatic · 60 marks. Documentation, scored by the system. Every criterion shows its points, whether it passed, and exactly why. Tap any line to override it — the override is recorded as yours.

Page 2 — Supervisor · 40 marks. Clinical reasoning 15, case discussion 10, professionalism 8, written reflection 7. All four are required.

Then press Submit mark to admin. You are not releasing it — the course administration does that. Nothing reaches the student until they do.

What the automatic score is, and is not. It measures whether the case is completely and consistently documented. It cannot judge whether the anaesthetic was appropriate. That is what your 40 marks are for. A well-conducted emergency case written up in ninety seconds will score poorly on documentation — use your marks and your feedback to say so.
صفحة ١ — آلية · ٦٠ درجة للتوثيق، ويمكنك تعديل أي معيار بالضغط عليه.
صفحة ٢ — ٤٠ درجة من عندك للحكم السريري، والأربعة مطلوبة.

الدرجة الآلية تقيس اكتمال التوثيق لا جودة التخدير. حالة طارئة أُديرت بإتقان وكُتبت على عجل ستأخذ درجة توثيق منخفضة — وضّح ذلك في درجاتك وملاحظاتك.

4 · Reviewing the marking scheme

On the dashboard, under Automatic Marking, press Review the marking scheme. It lists every criterion, its points, and in plain words what makes it pass. Read it before you rely on the scores.

If a criterion looks wrong to you, say so — the scheme is editable and was written to be argued with.

5 · Amending a mark

Reopen the case and press Save changes. You will be told what the mark was and what it will become. The old figure is kept in the record's history, so a mark that changed stays explainable.
