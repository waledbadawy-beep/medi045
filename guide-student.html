0 · Class access code — first time only

The first time you open the log book on a device, it asks for the class access code. Enter it once; you will not be asked again on that device.

Undergraduate class access code
MEDI045-UG-DU1C-KANA-LHBP

If you later open the log book on a second device — a college computer, another phone — it will ask again. Enter the same code.

Your cases are saved on the device you use. If a red bar appears saying records have not been uploaded, tap it; that usually means the code has not been entered on that device yet.

1 · Register — once only

  1. Open the log book link and complete the registration screen.
  2. Enter your full name and university ID. Both are required.
  3. Select your group (A1, A2, B1, B2, F1 or F2) and the academic year.
  4. Tap Start Logbook →.
Your university ID is what links every case to you in the supervisor panel. Type it exactly as it appears on your student card. A wrong ID means your cases will not appear under your name and cannot be marked.

2 · Your home screen

The home screen shows your Cases count, Skills count and your Target, plus any announcements posted by the programme. Check announcements at the start of each week — memos, regulations and deadlines are posted there.

3 · Log a case

Tap New Case Record and choose the setting you were in:

TypeWhat it records
🏥 Pre-operative Anaesthesia ClinicPre-op assessment, airway, LEMON, anaesthetic plan
⚕️ IntraoperativeTechnique, medications, fluids, events
🫁 PACU / Recovery RoomVitals, Aldrete score, discharge decision
🛏️ Ward / Pain RoundCLO 2.3, pain, WHO ladder, satisfaction
  1. Complete the form while the case is still fresh — same day if possible.
  2. Tap 💾 Save Draft if you need to finish later.
  3. To finish a draft, open 🗂️ Student Portfolio, tap the draft, then tap ✏️ Continue editing this draft. The form reopens with everything you had already entered.
  4. Tap ✓ Submit Case when it is complete. Once submitted it goes to your supervisor for marking.
Review everything before submitting. A submitted case is in the supervisor's queue and you should not expect to edit it yourself.
A draft is stored only on the device and browser you wrote it on. It is not sent to the server and is not in any backup. If you clear your browsing data, switch phone or switch browser, the draft is gone and cannot be recovered by anyone. Submit your cases rather than leaving them as drafts.

4 · Practical Sessions — Skill Lab

Thirteen stations, grouped into Airway Management, Vascular Access, Regional Anaesthesia, Equipment & Safety and Simulation & Communication — each with learning objectives and a step-by-step checklist. After each station, tap every step and rate yourself honestly:

  • ✓ Confident — performed it correctly and unaided
  • ~ Needs Practice — performed it partially or with help
  • ✗ Not Performed — not yet attempted

Record the date, the supervisor present, the number of attempts and any notes. Your KPI score builds from these ratings. Tap Save before leaving the station.

Marking a step "Confident" when you needed help removes the only signal your supervisor has that you need more time on it. Rate yourself accurately — the score is a teaching tool, not an exam.

6 · Programme evaluation

At the end of the rotation, open 📝 Program Evaluation and rate the workshops, teaching and resources, then tap 📨 Submit Evaluation. Your feedback shapes the next rotation.

8 · How your cases are marked

Each submitted case is scored out of 100:

  • 60 marks — documentation. Scored automatically from what you recorded: patient identity, ASA, monitoring, airway, ventilation, fluids, Aldrete, pain assessment and so on. Complete, consistent records score well.
  • 40 marks — clinical judgment. Awarded by your supervisor: reasoning, case discussion, professionalism, and the quality of your written reflection.

The automatic part measures how completely the case is documented, not whether the anaesthetic was right. That judgment is your supervisor's, and carries its own 40 marks.

A mark is not yours until the programme releases it. Your supervisor marks the case, then the course administration issues it. You will not see a score before that.

7 · Deadlines

The programme sets a submission window for each batch. You cannot submit before the start date or after the deadline. The dates are shown on your home screen — do not leave your cases to the final day.

📊 Supervisor Panel is not for students. It is password-protected and used by teaching staff to mark your work.

١ · التسجيل — مرة واحدة فقط

  1. افتح رابط السجل السريري وأكمل شاشة التسجيل.
  2. أدخل الاسم الكامل والرقم الجامعي، وكلاهما مطلوب.
  3. اختر المجموعة (A1 أو A2 أو B1 أو B2 أو F1 أو F2) والسنة الدراسية.
  4. اضغط Start Logbook →.
الرقم الجامعي هو ما يربط كل حالة باسمك في لوحة المشرف. أدخله تماماً كما هو في البطاقة الجامعية؛ فالرقم الخاطئ يعني أن حالاتك لن تظهر تحت اسمك ولن يمكن تقييمها.

٤ · الجلسات العملية — مختبر المهارات

ثلاث عشرة محطة، موزّعة على: إدارة المجرى الهوائي، والمداخل الوريدية، والتخدير الموضعي، والأجهزة والسلامة، والمحاكاة والتواصل — لكل منها أهداف تعليمية وقائمة خطوات. بعد كل محطة اضغط على كل خطوة وقيّم نفسك بصدق:

  • ✓ Confident — أدّيتها بشكل صحيح ودون مساعدة
  • ~ Needs Practice — أدّيتها جزئياً أو بمساعدة
  • ✗ Not Performed — لم تُجرَّب بعد

سجّل التاريخ والمشرف الحاضر وعدد المحاولات وأي ملاحظات. يُحتسب مؤشر أدائك KPI من هذه التقييمات. اضغط Save قبل مغادرة المحطة.

اختيار «Confident» لخطوة احتجت فيها إلى مساعدة يحرم مشرفك من المؤشر الوحيد الذي يخبره أنك تحتاج وقتاً أطول فيها. قيّم نفسك بدقة؛ فالدرجة أداة تعليمية وليست اختباراً.
