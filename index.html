Add it to your home screen

The log book works like an app once installed, and opens straight from your home screen.

  • iPhone / iPad (Safari): tap Share ⎙ → Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen.
Use the same device and the same browser for the whole rotation. Drafts are held on the device you wrote them on and are not copied to the server. Clearing your browsing data, switching phone or switching browser loses any draft permanently. Submit your cases rather than leaving them as drafts.

Trouble opening the log book?

  • Check that you are using the link above and not an older saved copy.
  • If the app looks out of date or a problem persists after it was reported fixed, force a refresh: Android Chrome — ⋮ → Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data → Cached images and files; iPhone Safari — close the tab and reopen the link; computer — Ctrl + Shift + R. A normal reload often keeps serving the stored copy.
  • On hospital Wi-Fi, if the page loads but nothing saves to the server, try mobile data once and tell your supervisor which network failed.
  • Cases submitted after the batch deadline are not accepted — check the deadline on your home screen.
